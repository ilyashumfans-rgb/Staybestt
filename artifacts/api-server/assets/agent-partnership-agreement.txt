3. PURPOSE OF AGREEMENT
The Company appoints the Agent as an independent business associate to promote Staybestt services, introduce property owners and generate eligible bookings through the Staybestt platform.

4. BUSINESS CATEGORIES
The Agent may introduce business partners under the following categories: Hotels, Resorts, Homestays, Villas, and Day Out activities and packages.

5. AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES
The Agent agrees to identify and introduce potential property owners; promote Staybestt services; generate genuine customer bookings; coordinate with vendors and customers when required; provide accurate information; follow company policies; maintain professional conduct; and protect the reputation and brand of Staybestt.

6. AGENT COMMISSION STRUCTURE
Agent Commission: {{commission}}

The Agent shall receive commission for eligible and successfully completed bookings generated through the Agent's registered referrals. Commission shall be calculated on the net commission revenue actually earned and received by Staybestt for the eligible booking, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

7. COMMISSION PAYMENT TERMS
1. Commission will be calculated after successful completion of the hotel stay or Day Out service.
2. Cancelled bookings will not qualify for commission.
3. Fully refunded bookings will not qualify for commission.
4. Commission will be processed monthly.
5. The Company will maintain booking and commission records.
6. Applicable TDS and other statutory deductions will be made.
7. Commission payments will be transferred to the Agent's registered bank account.

8. BOOKING AND CUSTOMER MANAGEMENT
All bookings must be processed through Staybestt's official website or application. Agents must not make unauthorised booking commitments, collect customer payments without written permission, or misuse customer information.

9. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Agent shall not disclose confidential company information, vendor details, customer information, commission structures or business strategies to unauthorised persons.

10. BRAND AND COMPANY REPRESENTATION
The Agent shall not misuse the Staybestt name or logo, make false promises to customers, offer unauthorised discounts, enter into agreements on behalf of Staybestt without written authorisation, or collect unauthorised payments.

11. NON-CIRCUMVENTION
The Agent shall not misuse company-provided vendor or customer information to bypass Staybestt or divert business opportunities for personal benefit.

12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
