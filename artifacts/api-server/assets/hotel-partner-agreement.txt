2. PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms under which the Hotel Partner will enroll its property with StayBestt and make rooms, rates, packages, and related accommodation services available for booking through the StayBestt website, application, and other authorized distribution channels.

The Parties intend to establish a mutually beneficial relationship in which StayBestt promotes and facilitates bookings for the Hotel Partner and the Hotel Partner provides the accommodation and services associated with confirmed reservations.

⸻

3. HOTEL ENROLLMENT

The Hotel Partner agrees to provide StayBestt with accurate and complete information required to create and maintain the property’s listing.

This may include:

* Hotel name and address
* Contact details
* Property description
* Room categories
* Room photographs
* Amenities and facilities
* Number of rooms available
* Room occupancy limits
* Meal plans
* Check-in and check-out times
* Cancellation policies
* Child and extra-bed policies
* Applicable taxes and fees
* Rates and promotional offers
* Hotel policies
* Required licenses and registrations

The Hotel Partner represents that it has the necessary authority to provide the information and offer the accommodation services through StayBestt.

⸻

4. HOTEL REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

The Hotel Partner represents and warrants that:

1. The information provided to StayBestt is accurate and current.
2. It has all licenses, registrations, permits, approvals, and insurance required to operate the property.
3. The property complies with applicable health, safety, fire, employment, consumer-protection, and hospitality requirements.
4. It has the authority to enter into this Agreement.
5. The rooms and services offered through StayBestt will be available as represented.
6. It will honor valid confirmed reservations made through StayBestt, subject to the applicable booking terms.
7. It will promptly notify StayBestt of any material change affecting availability, pricing, facilities, or the ability to honor reservations.

⸻

5. HOTEL LISTING AND CONTENT

The Hotel Partner grants StayBestt permission during the term of this Agreement to use the hotel’s name, logo, photographs, descriptions, trademarks, and other materials supplied by the Hotel Partner for the purpose of listing, marketing, promoting, and facilitating bookings for the property.

The Hotel Partner confirms that it has the necessary rights to provide such materials and that their authorized use by StayBestt will not knowingly infringe third-party rights.

StayBestt may format, resize, organize, or otherwise adapt supplied content for presentation on its website, application, and authorized marketing channels, provided that the underlying information is not intentionally misrepresented.

⸻

6. RATES AND ROOM INVENTORY

The Hotel Partner shall provide StayBestt with agreed room rates and inventory.

The Hotel Partner is responsible for ensuring that rates and availability supplied to StayBestt are accurate.

The Hotel Partner shall promptly update StayBestt regarding:

* Room availability
* Rate changes
* Blackout dates
* Closed dates
* Room closures
* Renovations
* Temporary service restrictions
* Changes to hotel facilities
* Changes to applicable policies

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the Hotel Partner shall honor the rate and room type displayed at the time a reservation is confirmed.

10. TAXES

11. BOOKING CONFIRMATION

A reservation made through StayBestt and confirmed through the StayBestt system shall constitute a valid booking subject to the applicable booking terms.

The Hotel Partner shall receive booking information through the agreed communication channel.

The Hotel Partner shall maintain accurate records of reservations and shall ensure that its staff are able to identify and process StayBestt reservations.

⸻

12. HONOURING CONFIRMED BOOKINGS

The Hotel Partner shall make reasonable arrangements to ensure that confirmed bookings are honored.

The Hotel Partner shall not knowingly cancel or refuse a confirmed reservation except where permitted by the booking terms, applicable law, or circumstances beyond its reasonable control.

If the Hotel Partner is unable to accommodate a confirmed guest, it shall immediately notify StayBestt and cooperate in arranging an appropriate alternative solution.

Any relocation, refund, compensation, or additional cost arising from a hotel-initiated inability to honor a confirmed booking shall be handled according to the applicable commercial terms and law.

⸻

13. OVERBOOKING

The Hotel Partner shall take reasonable measures to prevent overbooking.

If an overbooking occurs, the Hotel Partner shall immediately notify StayBestt and cooperate with StayBestt to minimize disruption to the guest.

Where the Hotel Partner is responsible for the overbooking, any reasonable costs or guest arrangements agreed between the Parties may be charged to the responsible Party, subject to applicable law and the agreed commercial terms.

⸻

14. CANCELLATION AND NO-SHOW POLICY

The cancellation and no-show rules applicable to each room, rate, or package shall be communicated to StayBestt before the relevant inventory is made available for booking.

The Hotel Partner agrees to honor the cancellation policy displayed to the guest at the time of booking.

Any modification to cancellation policies shall apply prospectively unless otherwise agreed.

⸻

15. HOTEL SERVICES AND GUEST EXPERIENCE

The Hotel Partner is solely responsible for providing the accommodation and hotel services associated with a reservation.

The Hotel Partner shall ensure that:

* Rooms are reasonably clean and maintained.
* Advertised facilities are available as represented, subject to disclosed restrictions.
* Guests receive the services included in their reservation.
* Applicable safety and legal requirements are followed.
* Hotel staff treat guests professionally and lawfully.
* Additional mandatory charges are disclosed as required by law.

⸻

16. GUEST COMPLAINTS

The Hotel Partner shall promptly investigate complaints relating to accommodation, facilities, hotel staff, cleanliness, safety, or services provided directly by the Hotel Partner.

StayBestt may assist in communicating with the guest but does not assume responsibility for services directly provided by the Hotel Partner.

The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve guest complaints efficiently.

⸻

17. HOTEL-IMPOSED CHARGES

Any mandatory charges applicable to a reservation should be clearly disclosed before booking where required by applicable law.

Optional charges, deposits, incidental charges, and additional services may be collected directly by the Hotel Partner where properly disclosed and legally permitted.

The Hotel Partner shall not impose undisclosed mandatory charges that contradict the booking terms provided to the guest.
